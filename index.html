Good to know

Frequently asked.

How long do the puddings stay fresh?
Keep them chilled and enjoy within 4 days for the crispiest crumble and freshest fruit.
Do you offer delivery?
Yes — same-day delivery in select areas, or swing by and pick up fresh from the studio. Delivery availability and fees are confirmed after you submit your order.
Are the puddings nut-free?
Most flavors are, except our Pistachio Banana. Check with us for full allergen information before ordering.
Can I order in bulk for events?
Absolutely — add your quantities to the cart and choose your date. We’ll confirm availability with you.
